# Feature Request: Notifications

**From:** Jamie Park (Product Manager)
**To:** Engineering Team
**Date:** Monday morning standup follow-up

---

Hey team,

We've been getting a lot of feedback from customers about order updates. Basically nobody knows what's happening with their orders after they place them, and support keeps getting calls asking "where's my stuff?"

Can we add notifications to the system? I'm thinking like — when things happen to an order, we let the customer know. Shipping updates, that kind of thing. Maybe also confirmation when they first place the order?

A few customers mentioned they'd prefer texts over emails, so let's make sure we handle that. Oh and one of the enterprise clients asked if we could do webhooks for their system — not sure how important that is but worth noting.

Some things to consider:
- We probably don't want to spam people. Maybe they can pick what they get?
- Our support team should probably be able to see what notifications went out for an order
- We'll want to make sure things actually get delivered. Had some issues with our email provider dropping stuff last quarter

I'd love to have at least something basic ready for the next sprint review. Let me know if you have questions!

Thanks,
Jamie

P.S. Sarah from marketing also mentioned she'd love to send promotional stuff through whatever we build, but that can probably wait.
